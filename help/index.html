Help Center

Quick answers for Lumina Archive on iPhone.

What is Lumina Archive?

A personal vault for links you care about — workouts, inspiration, anything you want to revisit. We store the link and a preview when we can. Watching always happens in the original app or browser.

How do I save a link?

  • In the app: tap +, paste a URL, pick a collection, Save.
  • Share Extension: in Safari (or another app), open Share → Lumina Archive. Then open Lumina Archive to finish saving if prompted.

Why is there no preview?

Some sites block Open Graph metadata. You can still save the link. Titles stay editable by saving with whatever title we could detect (often the hostname).

How do I watch something again?

Tap the card. Lumina Archive opens the source URL (and may hand off to an installed app such as YouTube when possible).

Collections

Create collections from the Collections tab. Rename or delete via long-press / context menu. A default “Saved” collection is created for you on first launch.

Archive+

Optional subscription for unlimited collections and saves, plus no ads. Purchase and restore through Apple. Manage in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Ads

Free users may see a banner on Home and Collections only. Report an ad from Settings if something looks wrong.

Privacy & accounts

No Lumina login. See the Privacy Policy for AdMob, Firebase, RevenueCat, CloudKit, and unfurling.
